Detailed Description

Handles GROUP_INFO packets. L3 under BaseAuthHandler.

Returns current group membership and roles to the requester. Also handles the "grant_admin" and "revoke_admin" sub-commands (only the group creator may grant/revoke admin status); on either, every other online member is fanned a fresh GROUP_INFO too, so anyone with the group info sheet open sees the role change live instead of only on next fetch.
