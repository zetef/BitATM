Detailed Description

Handles DELETE_CONVERSATION packets ("delete for everyone"). L3 under BaseAuthHandler.

Hard-deletes all messages between the sender and the peer named in to. The peer is notified immediately if online; otherwise a pending_notifications row is queued and delivered as the same packet on the peer's next login.
